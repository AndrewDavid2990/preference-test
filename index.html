      Deeper red means the row message won that matchup more often. Based only on pairs that were not skipped. A highlighted row is ranked above a message it lost to or tied with; the cell in question is outlined.

      Bradley-Terry strength scores, shown as each message's chance of beating an average message in this set. The pale band is a 90% interval from resampling participants: overlapping bands mean the messages are not clearly separated.

        Two checks on whether you can call a winner.

        Does the top message beat every other message head to head?

        Is first place clearly ahead of second?

        Where each message landed in every participant's personal ranking. A message that is loved by some and hated by others looks average on total wins but shows up here as a split.

          Two junk signals. Cycles: if a participant prefers A over B and B over C, they should prefer A over C; each violation is a cycle, and lots of them means random clicking or messages too similar to judge. Speed: the median time per pair, flagged as rushed under 1.5 seconds, which is too fast to have read two messages.
