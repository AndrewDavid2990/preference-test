        Two checks on whether you can call a winner.

        Is first place clearly ahead of second?

        Where each message landed in every participant's personal ranking. A message that is loved by some and hated by others looks average on total wins but shows up here as a split.

          If a participant prefers A over B and B over C, they should prefer A over C. Each violation is a cycle. Lots of cycles means random clicking or messages too similar to judge. Hide a participant to drop them from every other view. Hidden choices are remembered in this browser.
